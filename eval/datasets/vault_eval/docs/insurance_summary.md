# Commercial Vehicle Insurance: Summary of Cover

Policy HCS-88021 insures the fleet of Hollis Courier Services Ltd with Brackley Mutual Insurance, from 1 January to 31 December 2025.

## Cover

The policy provides comprehensive cover for 18 vans listed in the schedule, including third-party liability up to GBP 5,000,000 per incident.

## Excess

The standard excess is GBP 500 per claim. For drivers under the age of 25 the excess is GBP 1,000.

## Goods in Transit

Goods in transit are covered up to GBP 25,000 per vehicle. Cash, jewellery and mobile phones are excluded.

## Exclusions

The policy does not cover driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs, racing, or use of a vehicle outside the United Kingdom and the Republic of Ireland.

## Claims

Claims must be reported within forty-eight hours of an incident. The insurer aims to settle undisputed claims within twenty-one days.

## Premium

The annual premium is GBP 31,640, payable in twelve monthly instalments of GBP 2,636.67.
