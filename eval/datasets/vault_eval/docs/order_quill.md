# Subscription Order Form

Order Form QF-2291 is placed on 1 April 2024 by Marlowe Dental Group (the "Customer") with Quill Practice Software, Inc. (the "Provider") under the Provider's standard Terms of Service.

## 1. Subscription

The Customer subscribes to the Quill Scheduling and Billing suite for 120 named clinician seats at its ten clinics. Additional seats may be added at any time at the per-seat price below.

## 2. Pricing

The price is USD 38 per seat per month, billed annually in advance, for an annual subscription fee of USD 54,720. Implementation is a one-time fee of USD 6,000.

Prices exclude sales tax, which the Customer pays where applicable.

## 3. Term and Renewal

The subscription term is twelve months from 1 April 2024. It renews automatically for twelve-month terms unless the Customer gives sixty (60) days' written notice before the renewal date.

At renewal the Provider may raise the per-seat price by up to 6%.

## 4. Support

Support is included at the Standard level: email and chat support on business days from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Eastern time, with a first response target of four business hours.

Phone support and a named customer success manager are available only on the Premier level.

## 5. Data and Security

The Provider hosts Customer data in the United States and encrypts it in transit and at rest. The Provider shall keep backups for thirty-five days.

## 6. Data Export

On written request during the term and for ninety days afterwards, the Provider shall deliver a complete export of Customer data in CSV format at no charge.

## 7. Service Availability

The Provider targets 99.9% monthly uptime and credits 10% of one month's fees for each month in which uptime falls below 99.0%.

## 8. Order of Precedence

If this Order Form conflicts with the Terms of Service, this Order Form controls.
