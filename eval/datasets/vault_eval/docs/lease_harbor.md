# Commercial Office Lease

This Lease is made on 2 June 2023 between Pellam Properties LLC, as landlord, and Ostrander & Wills LLP, as tenant, for Suite 410 at 88 Harbor Street, Portland, Maine.

## 1. Premises and Term

The premises are approximately 3,100 rentable square feet on the fourth floor. The term is five (5) years, beginning on 1 September 2023.

The tenant may renew once for a further five years by giving the landlord at least nine months' written notice before the term ends.

## 2. Rent

Base rent is USD 12,400 per month, payable on the first day of each month without demand. Rent increases by 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date.

Rent paid more than five days late carries a late charge of 4% of the overdue amount.

## 3. Security Deposit

The tenant has paid a security deposit of USD 24,800. The landlord holds it as security for the tenant's obligations and shall return what remains within thirty days after the lease ends.

## 4. Operating Expenses

The tenant pays its proportionate share of building operating expenses and property taxes above the 2023 base year. The landlord shall give an annual statement within ninety days after each calendar year.

## 5. Use and Hours

The premises may be used only as professional offices. Building services are provided from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on weekdays and 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays.

## 6. Parking

The tenant receives six unreserved parking passes at USD 95 per pass per month.

## 7. Maintenance and Repairs

The landlord maintains the structure, roof and common areas. The tenant maintains its interior finishes and shall report any leak or damage promptly.

## 8. Alterations

The tenant needs the landlord's prior written consent for structural alterations, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Cosmetic changes need no consent.

## 9. Assignment and Subletting

The tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the premises without the landlord's written consent, except to an affiliate under common control.

## 10. Default

The tenant is in default if rent remains unpaid ten days after written notice, or if another breach is not cured within thirty days after written notice.

## 11. Insurance

The tenant shall carry commercial general liability insurance of at least USD 2,000,000 per occurrence and name the landlord as an additional insured.

Signed for Pellam Properties LLC by Gordon Pellam, Manager. Signed for Ostrander & Wills LLP by Ruth Ostrander, Managing Partner.
