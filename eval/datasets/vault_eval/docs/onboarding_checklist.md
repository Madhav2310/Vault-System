# Juniper Legal: New Starter Onboarding Checklist

This checklist is completed by the hiring manager and the new starter.

## Before the First Day

IT sets up the laptop and accounts at least three days before the start date. HR sends the contract and right-to-work checklist two weeks ahead.

## First Day

The new starter arrives at 9:30 a.m. at reception and meets the hiring manager. A buddy is assigned and takes the starter to lunch.

## First Week

The new starter completes the information security course and the conflict-of-interest declaration by Friday. They also book a thirty-minute call with each member of the team.

## First Month

The starter agrees three objectives with the manager by the end of week four and attends the firm-wide induction session on the first Thursday of the month.

## Probation

Probation lasts six months. Reviews take place at the end of months three and six, and either party may end employment on one week's notice during probation.

## Equipment

Laptops are returned to IT on the last day of employment. Lost equipment must be reported within one working day.
