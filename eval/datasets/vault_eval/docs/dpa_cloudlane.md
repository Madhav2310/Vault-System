# Data Processing Addendum

This Data Processing Addendum forms part of the hosting agreement between Cloudlane Hosting B.V. (the "Processor") and Yarrow Retail Group (the "Controller"), dated 20 February 2025.

## 1. Scope

The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller only to provide the hosting services and only on the Controller's documented instructions.

## 2. Security

The Processor shall maintain encryption of personal data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256. Access is limited to staff who need it, and access logs are kept for twelve months.

## 3. Sub-processors

The Processor may engage sub-processors listed in Annex 1. It shall give the Controller thirty days' notice of any new sub-processor, and the Controller may object on reasonable grounds within that period.

## 4. Breach Notification

The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two hours after becoming aware of a personal data breach.

## 5. International Transfers

Personal data stays within the European Economic Area. Transfers elsewhere need the Controller's prior written consent and an approved transfer mechanism.

## 6. Audits

The Controller may audit the Processor's compliance once per year on fourteen days' notice, or at any time after a breach. The Processor shall accept a current ISO 27001 certificate as evidence for the technical measures.

## 7. Deletion

Within thirty days after the hosting agreement ends, the Processor shall delete or return all personal data, at the Controller's choice, and confirm the deletion in writing.
