# Q3 Sales Summary: Finance

Prepared by Finance, 14 October 2025. Source: general ledger close.

Revenue for the third quarter was USD 4.82 million, up 7% on the second quarter.

Finance counts 61 new customer accounts opened in the quarter, using the date of the first paid invoice.

Customer churn was 2.1% for the quarter.

Gross margin was 63%, helped by lower hosting costs after the migration in July.

Deferred revenue at quarter end stood at USD 1.9 million.
