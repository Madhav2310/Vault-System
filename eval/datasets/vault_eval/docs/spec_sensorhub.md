# Sensor Hub S2: Technical Specification

The Sensor Hub S2 collects readings from wireless environmental sensors and forwards them to the Cedarline cloud.

## Connectivity

The hub supports Wi-Fi 6 on 2.4 and 5 GHz, Ethernet at 1 Gbps and Bluetooth 5.2. It can pair with up to 64 sensors at once.

## Power

The hub is powered by a 12 V DC adapter drawing at most 9 W. A built-in battery keeps it running for six hours during a power cut.

## Environmental Limits

Operating temperature ranges from minus 10 to 50 degrees Celsius. Humidity up to 90% non-condensing is supported. The enclosure is rated IP54.

## Physical

The hub measures 120 by 80 by 30 millimetres and weighs 310 grams. It mounts to a wall or DIN rail.

## Data

Readings are stored locally for seven days if the cloud is unreachable. Data sent to the cloud is encrypted with TLS 1.3.

## Certifications

The S2 carries CE and FCC marks and is RoHS compliant. It has not been certified for use in explosive atmospheres.
