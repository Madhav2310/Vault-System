# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 14 January 2025 between Northwind Logistics Ltd, a company registered in England (the "Customer"), and Tessellate Systems Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Supplier").

## 1. Definitions

"Services" means the warehouse routing software, hosting and support described in each Statement of Work signed under this Agreement. "Confidential Information" means non-public business, technical or financial information disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally or in writing.

## 2. Term

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months, unless it is ended earlier under Section 9.

After the initial term the Agreement renews for successive twelve-month periods unless either party gives ninety days' written notice of non-renewal.

## 3. Fees and Payment

The Customer shall pay the Supplier a monthly platform fee of USD 18,500, invoiced in advance on the first business day of each month.

Undisputed invoices are payable within forty-five days of receipt. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

The Supplier may raise the platform fee once per contract year by no more than 4%, with sixty days' notice.

## 4. Service Levels

The Supplier shall keep the platform available for at least 99.5% of each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.

If availability falls below that level, the Customer receives a service credit of 5% of the monthly platform fee for each full percentage point of shortfall.

Scheduled maintenance is limited to eight hours per month and shall be announced at least five business days in advance.

## 5. Data Protection

The Supplier shall process Customer personal data only on documented instructions and shall notify the Customer of a personal data breach within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.

Customer data is stored in data centres located in Ireland and the Netherlands.

## 6. Intellectual Property

Each party keeps its pre-existing intellectual property. Routing models trained only on Customer data belong to the Customer, and the Supplier receives a licence to use them solely to provide the Services.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with at least the care it gives its own, and shall use it only to perform this Agreement. These duties continue for three (3) years after the Agreement ends.

## 8. Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Each party's total liability in any contract year is limited to the fees paid in the preceding twelve months, except for breach of confidentiality or wilful misconduct.

## 9. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other commits a material breach and fails to cure it within thirty (30) days after written notice.

The Customer may terminate for convenience on one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice.

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of London have exclusive jurisdiction.
