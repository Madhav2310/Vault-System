# Fenwick & Marsh Travel Booking Guide

This guide explains how staff at Fenwick & Marsh LLP book business travel through the Meridian travel portal.

## Booking Flights

Flights should be booked at least fourteen days before departure to qualify for the negotiated fares. Bookings made inside that window need approval from a partner.

## Changing and Cancelling

Tickets booked through the portal can be changed free of charge up to twenty-four hours before departure. After that, the airline's change fee applies and is charged to the matter code.

The firm has a preferred programme with the Larkspur hotel group, which gives a 12% discount and free breakfast at its city-centre properties.

## Expenses After the Trip

Staff file expenses in the portal within ten days of returning. Receipts are photographed and attached to each line.
