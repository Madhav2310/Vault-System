# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (Version 2)

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into on 21 February 2025 by Brightwater Biosciences, Inc. ("Brightwater") and Corvane Partners LP ("Corvane"), together the "Parties", to allow them to evaluate a possible investment in Brightwater's oncology programme.

## 1. Purpose

The Parties will exchange information solely to evaluate the proposed investment (the "Purpose"). Neither Party is obliged to proceed with any transaction.

## 2. Confidential Information

Confidential Information covers trial data, formulations, manufacturing processes, cap tables, financial models and the existence of the discussions. It excludes information that is public, already known to the recipient, independently developed, or received from a third party without restriction.

## 3. Obligations

The recipient shall keep Confidential Information secret, restrict access to employees and advisers who need it for the Purpose, and bind those people to confidentiality terms no less strict than these.

The recipient may disclose Confidential Information if a court or regulator requires it, provided the recipient gives prompt written notice where the law allows.

## 4. Term and Survival

The obligations of confidentiality last for five (5) years from the date of last disclosure under this Agreement.

Trade secrets stay protected for as long as they remain trade secrets, regardless of the period above.

## 5. Return of Materials

Within ten days of a written request, the recipient shall return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify the destruction in writing. One archival copy may be kept by its legal department to prove compliance.

## 6. No Licence

Nothing in this Agreement grants a licence to any patent, copyright or other right. All Confidential Information remains the property of the disclosing Party.

## 7. Remedies

Each Party agrees that breach may cause irreparable harm and that the other Party may seek an injunction without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy.

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and the Parties submit to the courts located in Wilmington.

## 9. General

This is the entire agreement on its subject. It may be changed only in a writing signed by both Parties. Neither Party may assign it without the other's written consent.

Signed for Brightwater Biosciences, Inc. by Priya Raman, Chief Financial Officer. Signed for Corvane Partners LP by Jonas Eklund, Managing Partner.
