# Pinewood Fitness App: Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 May 2025. Pinewood Fitness Ltd is the controller of personal data collected through the app.

## Data We Collect

We collect your name, email address, date of birth, workout history and, if you allow it, heart-rate data from your wearable device. We do not collect precise location.

## How We Use It

We use your data to run your account, personalise workout plans and send service messages. We send marketing emails only if you opt in.

## Sharing

We share data with cloud hosting providers and with our payment processor. We do not sell personal data.

## Retention

Account data is kept while your account is open. After you delete your account we remove it within sixty days, except invoices, which we keep for seven years for tax reasons.

## Your Rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, and to export it in a machine-readable format. We respond within thirty days.

## Children

The app is not intended for anyone under sixteen, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Contact

Write to the Data Protection Officer at privacy@pinewoodfitness.example.
