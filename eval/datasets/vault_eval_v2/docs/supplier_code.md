# Bramble & Rowe Supplier Code of Conduct

Bramble & Rowe Ltd expects all suppliers to follow this code. Suppliers must also make sure their own suppliers do so.

## Labour

Suppliers shall not use forced, bonded or child labour. The minimum working age is fifteen, or the local legal minimum if higher. Workers shall be paid at least the legal minimum wage.

## Working Hours

Regular working hours may not exceed forty-eight per week, and total hours including overtime may not exceed sixty per week. Workers must have at least one rest day in seven.

## Health and Safety

Suppliers shall provide a safe workplace, fire exits that are never blocked, and drinking water. Serious accidents must be reported to Bramble & Rowe within five days.

## Environment

Suppliers shall comply with environmental law, keep records of waste and emissions, and work to reduce them each year.

## Anti-Corruption

Suppliers shall not offer or accept bribes. Gifts to Bramble & Rowe staff above GBP 50 in value are not allowed.

## Audits and Remedies

Bramble & Rowe may audit any site with ten working days' notice. A supplier that fails an audit must submit a corrective action plan within thirty days.
