# Orchard Analytics Employee Handbook: Leave and Time Off

This chapter applies to all full-time employees of Orchard Analytics Ltd. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata.

## Annual Leave

Employees receive twenty-five days of paid annual leave per leave year, plus the public holidays of their country. The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

Up to five unused days may be carried into the next leave year and must be taken by 30 June.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive full pay for up to ten days of sick leave in a rolling twelve-month period. A doctor's note is required after three consecutive days of absence.

## Parental Leave

The primary carer receives sixteen weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary carer receives four weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within six months of the birth or adoption.

Employees must tell their manager at least fifteen weeks before the expected due date.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to five days of paid leave on the death of a spouse, child or parent, and up to two days for other close relatives.

## Sabbatical

After five years of service, employees may apply for an unpaid sabbatical of up to three months. Requests need director approval and at least four months' notice.

## Requesting Leave

Leave is requested in the people system. Managers answer within three working days. Requests of more than ten consecutive days need director approval.
