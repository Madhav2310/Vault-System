# Business Internet Service Level Agreement

Fibrenet Connectivity Ltd provides dedicated internet access to Harcourt Print Works under Service Order 7714, effective 1 March 2025.

## 1. Service

The service is a symmetric 1 Gbps fibre line with a static block of eight IPv4 addresses. The committed information rate is 1 Gbps at all times.

## 2. Availability

Fibrenet commits to 99.95% monthly availability, measured at the customer premises equipment and excluding planned maintenance.

## 3. Credits

If availability in a month is below 99.95% but at least 99.5%, the customer receives a credit of 10% of the monthly charge. Below 99.5% but at least 99.0%, the credit is 25%. Below 99.0%, the credit is 50%.

Credits must be claimed within thirty days after the end of the month concerned.

## 4. Repair Times

Fibrenet shall restore a total loss of service within four hours of a fault report during business hours and within eight hours outside them. A partial degradation shall be resolved within one business day.

## 5. Planned Maintenance

Planned maintenance occurs between 01:00 and 05:00 on Sundays, with at least seven days' notice, and may not exceed six hours in any month.

## 6. Charges

The monthly charge is GBP 940. There is a one-time installation charge of GBP 1,500. The minimum term is thirty-six months, and early termination costs 50% of the remaining monthly charges.
