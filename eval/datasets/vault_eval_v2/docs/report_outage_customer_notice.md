# Customer Notice: Login Disruption on 9 September

Issued by Customer Support, 10 September 2025.

Some customers were unable to sign in for 62 minutes on the morning of 9 September.

The disruption affected the EU-West and EU-Central regions.

The cause was an expired TLS certificate on our identity gateway. We have since fixed the renewal process.

Customers on the Business and Enterprise plans will receive a service credit automatically. No action is needed.

We apologise for the disruption.
