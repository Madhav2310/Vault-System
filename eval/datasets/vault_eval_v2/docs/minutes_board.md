# Greenfield Housing Association: Minutes of the Board Meeting of 24 September 2025

Present: Joan Whitaker (chair), Samir Patel, Louisa Grant, Anders Holm, Fatima Yusuf. Apologies: Peter Roy.

## Minutes and Matters Arising

The minutes of 25 June 2025 were approved without change.

## Finance Report

The treasurer reported that the association held GBP 2.3 million in reserves at the end of August. Rent arrears stood at 3.8% of annual rent, down from 4.4% in June.

## Maintenance Contract

The board approved awarding the three-year repairs contract to Eastbrook Property Services at GBP 640,000 per year. The alternative bid from Calder Maintenance was GBP 705,000 per year.

## Development Pipeline

Anders Holm reported that planning permission for the Mill Lane site (48 homes) is expected in November. Construction would begin in March 2026 if funding is confirmed.

## Policy Review

The board approved the revised complaints policy. The target for responding to a formal complaint was reduced from twenty working days to ten.

## Next Meeting

The next meeting will take place on 10 December 2025.
