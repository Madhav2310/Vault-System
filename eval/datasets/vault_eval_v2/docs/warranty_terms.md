# Norden Appliances: Limited Warranty

This warranty covers Norden household appliances bought new from an authorised retailer.

## Coverage Periods

Dishwashers, ovens and refrigerators carry a warranty of two years on parts and labour. The compressor of a refrigerator is covered for ten years on parts only.

Small appliances such as kettles and toasters carry a one-year warranty.

## What Is Not Covered

The warranty does not cover cosmetic damage, consumables such as filters, damage from incorrect installation, or commercial use.

## Making a Claim

Contact Norden Customer Care with the model number, serial number and proof of purchase. A technician visit is arranged within five working days.

## Remedies

Norden will repair the appliance or, at its choice, replace it. A repaired appliance keeps its original warranty period; a replacement receives a new twelve-month warranty.

## Transfer

The warranty passes to a new owner if the appliance is sold within the warranty period and proof of purchase is handed over.
