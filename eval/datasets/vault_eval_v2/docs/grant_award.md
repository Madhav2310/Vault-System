# Award Letter: Research Grant MSF-2025-0318

Meridian Science Foundation is pleased to award Dr. Elena Varga of Lakeshore University a research grant for the project "Low-cost sensors for river nutrient monitoring".

## Amount and Period

The total award is USD 420,000 over thirty months, from 1 July 2025 to 31 December 2027. Funds are released in three instalments of USD 140,000 on 1 July 2025, 1 July 2026 and 1 July 2027.

## Allowable Costs

Indirect costs are limited to 15% of direct costs. Equipment over USD 5,000 per item needs prior written approval from the Foundation. Travel is allowed for up to two conferences per year.

## Reporting

A short progress report is due every six months. A final report and a data management summary are due within ninety days after the end date.

## Data Sharing

Data from the project must be deposited in a public repository within twelve months of collection, except where privacy or landowner agreements forbid it.

## Changes and Termination

Changes of principal investigator or of the project scope need written approval. The Foundation may suspend funding if reports are more than sixty days late.

Authorised for the Foundation by Hugo Brandt, Programme Director.
