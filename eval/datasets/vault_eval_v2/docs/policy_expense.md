# Employee Travel and Expense Policy

Policy owner: Finance Operations, Alder & Finch Design Studio. Effective 1 January 2024. This policy applies to every employee and contractor who is reimbursed for business expenses.

## 1. Principles

Spend company money as you would your own. Choose the reasonable option, not the cheapest or the most convenient. Expenses must have a business purpose that you can explain to your manager.

## 2. Meals

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to USD 60 per day in total. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at client dinners approved in advance by a director.

## 3. Lodging

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to USD 220 per night in most cities and USD 310 per night in New York, San Francisco and London. Book through the company travel portal where possible.

## 4. Air and Rail Travel

Economy fares are the default for flights under six hours. Flights over six hours may be booked in premium economy with manager approval. Rail travel is standard class.

## 5. Ground Transport

Use public transport, a taxi or a ride-share service for local trips. Rental cars need approval and must be the smallest practical class. Personal car mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS rate.

## 6. Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every expense of USD 25 or more. For smaller amounts a brief note in the expense report is enough.

## 7. Submitting Reports

Submit expense reports within thirty (30) days after the expense is incurred. Reports submitted later need written approval from the department head and may be declined.

## 8. Approval

Managers approve reports within five business days. Finance reimburses approved reports in the next weekly payment run.

## 9. Company Cards

Cards are issued to employees who travel more than six times a year. Personal expenses on a company card must be repaid within fourteen days.

## 10. Questions

Ask Finance Operations at expenses@alderfinch.example before you spend if you are unsure whether something is reimbursable.
