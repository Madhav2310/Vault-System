# Q3 Sales Summary: Regional Sales

Prepared by the Regional Sales team, 16 October 2025. Source: CRM bookings export.

Booked revenue for the third quarter was USD 4.68 million, up 4% on the second quarter.

The team counts 57 new customer accounts won in the quarter, using the date the contract was signed.

Customer churn was 2.1% for the quarter.

The strongest region was the Nordics, which contributed 22% of bookings.

The pipeline for the fourth quarter stands at USD 7.3 million, weighted.
