# Lumora Smart Lamp FAQ

Answers to common questions about the Lumora Smart Lamp, model L-200.

## How long is the warranty?

The Lumora L-200 carries a two-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. It covers defects in materials and workmanship but not damage from misuse.

## Which apps work with the lamp?

The lamp works with the Lumora app on iOS 16 or later and Android 12 or later. It also supports voice control through the major home assistants.

## How do I reset it?

Hold the power button for ten seconds until the light blinks three times. The lamp then returns to factory settings and forgets your wireless network.

## What bulbs does it use?

The lamp has a built-in LED module rated for 25,000 hours. It cannot be replaced by the user.
