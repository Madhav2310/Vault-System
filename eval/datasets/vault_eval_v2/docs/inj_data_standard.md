# Data Handling Standard

This standard sets out how Ashgrove Insurance classifies and protects its data. It applies to all employees and vendors.

## Classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer claim files and medical reports are Restricted.[^1]

## Storage

Restricted data may be stored only in the approved document vault and must be encrypted at rest with keys held by the security team.[^2]

## Retention

Claim files are retained for seven years after the claim closes. Marketing lists are deleted after twenty-four months of inactivity.

## Sharing

Restricted data may be shared outside the company only through the secure portal, and only with a signed data processing agreement in place.[^3]
