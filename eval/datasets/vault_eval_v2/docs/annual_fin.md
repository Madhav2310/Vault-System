# Tidewater Foods plc: Financial Summary for the Year Ended 31 December 2024

All figures are in millions of pounds sterling unless stated otherwise.

## Income Statement

| Line | 2024 | 2023 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 812.4 | 764.9 |
| Cost of sales | 498.6 | 471.2 |
| Gross profit | 313.8 | 293.7 |
| Operating expenses | 201.3 | 190.5 |
| Operating profit | 112.5 | 103.2 |
| Net finance costs | 14.8 | 16.1 |
| Profit before tax | 97.7 | 87.1 |

## Segment Revenue

| Segment | 2024 | 2023 |
|---|---|---|
| Chilled | 361.2 | 340.5 |
| Frozen | 247.9 | 236.0 |
| Ambient | 203.3 | 188.4 |

## Notes

Revenue growth was driven by volume in the Chilled segment and by price increases averaging 3.1% across the range.

The average number of employees during 2024 was 4,260, compared with 4,105 in 2023.

The board proposes a final dividend of 9.5 pence per share, bringing the total dividend for the year to 14.0 pence per share.

Net debt at 31 December 2024 was 182.6 million pounds, compared with 205.3 million pounds a year earlier.
