# Request for Proposals: Campus Catering Services

Westbrook College invites proposals for catering services across its main campus for the academic years 2026 to 2029.

## Timeline

Questions are accepted until 6 February 2026. Proposals are due by 5:00 p.m. on 27 February 2026. The college expects to announce the award on 31 March 2026.

## Scope

The contractor will operate the central dining hall, two cafes and event catering. The dining hall serves about 1,800 meals per day during term.

## Requirements

Bidders must show at least five years of experience in education catering. At least 30% of food spend must come from suppliers within 150 miles of the campus. All menus must identify the fourteen major allergens.

## Pricing

Proposals must state a price per meal for the board plan and a management fee as a percentage of revenue. The college will not consider management fees above 8%.

## Evaluation

Proposals are scored out of 100: food quality and menus 35, price 30, sustainability 20 and experience 15.

## Submission

Submit one electronic copy to the Procurement Office. Late proposals will not be considered.
