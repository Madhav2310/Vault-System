# Quarterly Headcount Report: People Team

Prepared by the People Team, 3 October 2025.

Total headcount at 30 September was 412 employees, including 14 part-time staff.

Annualised attrition for the trailing twelve months was 9.4%.

There are 23 open requisitions, of which 11 are in Engineering.

The average time to fill a role was 52 days.

Employee engagement scored 78 out of 100 in the September pulse survey.
