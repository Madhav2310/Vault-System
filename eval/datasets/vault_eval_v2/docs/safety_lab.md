# Kestrel Materials: Laboratory Safety Procedure SOP-014

This procedure covers work with solvents and acids in Laboratory B.

## Personal Protective Equipment

Staff must wear safety glasses, a lab coat and nitrile gloves at all times in the lab. Face shields are required when pouring more than one litre of acid.

## Chemical Storage

Flammable solvents are kept in the grey cabinet and may not exceed fifty litres in total. Acids are stored below eye level in the yellow cabinet, away from bases.

## Spills

Small spills under 500 millilitres may be cleaned with the spill kit by trained staff. Larger spills require evacuation of the room and a call to the safety officer on extension 4410.

## Waste

Halogenated and non-halogenated waste go in separate labelled containers. Containers are collected on Tuesdays and must be sealed and dated.

## Training

Every new staff member completes the safety induction before entering the lab and refresher training every twelve months. Records are kept by the safety officer, Nadia Petrov.

## Incidents

All incidents, including near misses, are reported within twenty-four hours using the incident form.
