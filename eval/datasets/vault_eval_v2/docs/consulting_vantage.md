# Consulting Services Agreement

This Consulting Services Agreement is made on 6 October 2025 between Pinecrest Health Network (the "Client") and Vantage Analytics LLC (the "Consultant").

## 1. Services

The Consultant shall analyse the Client's outpatient scheduling data and deliver a capacity model and a recommendations report, as detailed in Schedule A.

## 2. Term

The term of this Agreement is twelve (12) months from the date above.

## 3. Fees

The Client shall pay the Consultant USD 140 per hour for time worked, invoiced monthly. Travel is billed at cost with prior approval.

Invoices are due thirty days after receipt. The Client shall not be charged for more than 600 hours without a written change order.

## 4. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the other.

## 5. Independent Contractor

The Consultant is an independent contractor. Nothing here makes the Consultant an employee, partner or agent of the Client.

## 6. Confidentiality and Patient Data

The Consultant shall not access identifiable patient records. All datasets are supplied de-identified, and the Consultant shall not attempt to re-identify any person.

## 7. Deliverables and Ownership

The Client owns the final report and model. The Consultant keeps its general methods and tools.

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.
