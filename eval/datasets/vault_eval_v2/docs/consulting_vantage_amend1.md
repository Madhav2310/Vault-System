# Amendment No. 1 to Consulting Services Agreement

This Amendment No. 1 is made on 19 January 2026 between Pinecrest Health Network and Vantage Analytics LLC and changes the Consulting Services Agreement of 6 October 2025.

## 1. Hourly Rate

From 1 February 2026 the Consultant's rate is USD 165 per hour.

## 2. Extended Term

The term of the Agreement runs for eighteen (18) months from 6 October 2025.

## 3. Notice of Termination

Either party may end the Agreement on forty-five (45) days' written notice.

## 4. Additional Services

The Consultant shall also produce a quarterly dashboard of appointment no-show rates, at the hourly rate above.

## 5. Effect of Amendment

Sections 1 and 2 of this Amendment replace the corresponding provisions of the Agreement. All other provisions of the Agreement continue unchanged except as the parties agree in writing.

Signed for Pinecrest Health Network by Dr. Alma Reuter, Chief Operating Officer. Signed for Vantage Analytics LLC by Ines Carvalho, Principal.
